## Posting & reach tips (non-premium)

How to make this land, from a free account:

- **Add to the "Licenses & Certifications" profile section too** — feed posts fade, the profile section persists and is what recruiters search. Use the verify URL as the "Credential URL" and `UPEX Galaxy` as the issuing org.
- **Tag `@UPEX Galaxy` (their company page)** in the post — it surfaces you to their followers and they may reshare, which is the single biggest free reach lever.
- **Post with an image** (the certificate as a PNG/JPEG, not just text) — image posts reliably outperform text-only.
- **Keep outbound links out of the body where possible** — LinkedIn downranks posts carrying external links. Keep the verify/portfolio link once or drop it into the first comment to protect reach.
- **3–5 focused hashtags** (already done above) — niche + broad mix, never 10+ spam.
- **Engage the first hour** — reply to every comment fast; early engagement is the strongest algorithmic signal.
- **End with a question/CTA** — comments drive reach far more than likes, so invite a reply (e.g. "who else is making the agentic QA shift?").
- **Post mid-week, morning** in your audience's timezone (Tue–Thu, ~8–10am).
- **Complete your profile** (All-Star) and turn on **Creator mode** — both free and both lift distribution.
- **No Premium needed** — Premium doesn't buy organic reach; consistent posting + engaging on others' content (reciprocity) does.
